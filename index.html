ftextarea

A simple, distraction-free textarea for quick notes and temporary text storage.

Why?

Sometimes you just need a place to paste a link or jot down a thought. Most apps require login, sync to the cloud, or format your text automatically. ftextarea does none of that — it's just a textarea.

How it works

  • Your text is saved automatically to your browser's localStorage
  • Works across tabs — switch tabs and your text stays in sync
  • Works offline after the first visit
  • No account needed, no data leaves your browser

Note: Browsers may clear localStorage periodically. This is designed for temporary, throwaway text — not long-term storage.

Open Source

This project is MIT licensed and open source. Built with vanilla HTML, CSS, and Rust compiled to WebAssembly.
